IdeaBook

A private notebook for Android. No account, no server, no tracking.

Everything you write stays on your phone. There is no account to create and nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you ask for it — and when you do, it goes to your own Google Drive, encrypted with a passphrase only you have.

What it does

Notes inside notesAny note can hold other notes, as deep as you like. Use it the way you would use folders, or ignore it and keep everything flat.
Write properlyHeadings, bullets, numbered lists, checkboxes, bold, italic, highlight and inline code. Tick a checklist item and it drops to the bottom, so a shopping list tidies itself as you shop.
Photos and tablesTake a photo straight into a note, or build a table with totals that add themselves up. Share a table as a picture, so it arrives readable instead of collapsing into a row of pipes.
Reminders that follow the noteOn a whole note, or on a single line inside one. Rename the note and the reminder says the new words.
Backups you controlExport an archive wherever you like, or switch on Drive backup. Set a passphrase and the archive is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM before it goes anywhere.
Lock it if you want toRequire your phone's PIN, pattern or fingerprint before the app opens. Off by default — it asks once, and you can say no.

What it does not do

There is no analytics library in the app, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting and no third-party tracker of any kind. Not "we do not use the data we collect" — there is no code in the app capable of collecting it. The one network connection it can make is to Google Drive, and only when you ask for a backup.

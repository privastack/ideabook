Privacy Policy for IdeaBook

Last updated: 27 September 2026

IdeaBook does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no server that belongs to us, and no analytics. Everything you write stays on your phone unless you deliberately send it somewhere.

What the app stores, and where

Your notes, photos, tables, tags and reminders are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. That area is not readable by other apps. We never receive a copy, and we have no way to read it.

Information we collect

None. The developer receives no personal information, no usage data, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers and no device identifiers. The app contains no analytics library, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker of any kind.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Camera — only when you take a photo for a note. Photos are written to the app's private storage. The camera is never used in the background.
  • Notifications — to show the reminders you set. Reminder notifications are marked private, so their contents stay hidden on your lock screen.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) — so a reminder set for 9:00 pm arrives at 9:00 pm rather than whenever the system next wakes up. If you decline this permission the app still works, with reminders delivered approximately.
  • Run at startup — your reminders are saved on the device and are never lost. However, Android itself discards pending alarms when the phone restarts, so the app sets them again after a reboot to make sure a reminder still arrives. Nothing else happens at startup, and the app does not keep running in the background.
  • Internet — used only if you switch on Google Drive backup. With that switched off, the app makes no network connections at all.
  • Vibration — to buzz when a reminder fires.

Google Drive backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Drive backup, the app uploads an archive of your diary to your own Google Drive, into a hidden folder reserved for this app (drive.appdata). Points worth being clear about:

  • The archive goes from your phone directly to Google. It does not pass through any server belonging to the developer, because no such server exists.
  • The developer cannot see, download or delete these backups. They are in your Drive, under your Google account.
  • The app requests the narrowest scope available. It cannot see, read or alter any other file in your Drive.
  • You can set a passphrase, in which case the archive is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is uploaded. Without that passphrase, nobody — including us — can read its contents.
  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Data handled by Google is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and exporting

When you share a note or a table, or export an archive to a location you choose, that content goes wherever you send it. The app has no part in what happens afterwards.

Children

IdeaBook is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Security

Notes are kept in app-private storage. You can require your phone's PIN, pattern or fingerprint before the app opens; while that lock is on, the app is hidden from the task switcher and cannot be screenshotted. Backups can be encrypted with a passphrase you choose. The app is excluded from Android's own cloud backup so that your diary is never copied off the device by the platform.

Deleting your data

Deleted notes go to the trash and are removed permanently when you empty it. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device. Drive backups, if you made any, are deleted from within the app's Settings or from your Google account.
